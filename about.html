Crafting Iconic Identities

Since 2020
Tampa Bay, St. Pete and Gulf Beaches

Lucid Reels Media gives Tampa Bay agents and brokerages everything a listing needs to stand out, from MLS-ready HDR photography and cinematic video to drone coverage and immersive virtual tours, all delivered fast and ready to post. Every shoot is planned around one goal: making your property the one buyers remember.

Our work moves numbers, not just impressions. Faster closings, packed open houses, and content that wins the next listing appointment. Every frame is color graded, stabilized, and edited to a cinematic standard, so your brand looks premium everywhere it appears.

Work Flow

01

Plan

Every shoot starts with your listing and your timeline. We learn the property's best features, the buyer it should attract, and coordinate with sellers, staging, and light to schedule the perfect window. You show up to a shoot that is already planned to sell.

02

Shoot

On shoot day we capture it all: MLS-ready HDR photography, cinematic video, agent-led walkthroughs, and FPV drone coverage. Every angle is composed to make rooms feel bright, open, and worth the showing.

03

Deliver

Photos back in 24 hours, video close behind. Everything arrives edited, color graded, and sized for MLS, Instagram, and YouTube, so you can market the listing while the competition is still waiting on their photographer.
